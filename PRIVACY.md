# Lasso privacy policy

_Last updated: November 3, 2026_

Lasso is an Android app that makes the text on your screen selectable. This policy explains what it does with your data.

## What Lasso accesses

When you open Lasso (with the accessibility button, a back-tap gesture, or the button in the app), it uses Android's accessibility service to:

- take one screenshot of the current screen,
- recognize the text in that screenshot on your phone, using Google's ML Kit text recognizer bundled in the app,
- watch for the Back key while Lasso is open, so Back closes it.

Lasso does nothing with the screen while it's closed.

## What Lasso stores and sends

- The screenshot and the recognized text stay in memory only while Lasso is open, and are discarded when you close it. Nothing is written to storage.
- Lasso has no internet permission, so it can't send anything anywhere. It has no ads, analytics or accounts.
- Text you copy goes to Android's clipboard. Text you share or translate goes to the app you choose, under that app's privacy policy.

The only thing Lasso saves is your settings, like whether to pause media while selecting. They stay on your phone.

## Children

Lasso isn't directed at children under 13.
